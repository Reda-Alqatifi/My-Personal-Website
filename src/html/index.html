About Me

My name is Reda

I am a Computer Science student with an interest in technology especially web development and software engineering. I enjoy learning how software works and building things by myself. Over time, I have learned different programming languages and tools, and I am always trying to improve my skills through practice and personal projects.

My main interest is Backend Development, but I also enjoy working on the frontend and building complete web applications. I like solving problems and finding better ways to build my projects. I believe that learning by building real projects is one of the best ways to improve my skills.

Programming is not my only interest. I also enjoy writing novels and drawing arts. For novels, I like to create stories with mystery, psychological, drama, and fantasy elements. For arts, I like to draw characters fictional and realistic, hand and digital drawing. In the future, I hope to keep improving my programming, writing, and drawing skills and continue creating projects that represent my interests.
